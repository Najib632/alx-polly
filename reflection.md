# Reflections on Building with AI

This project has been an in-depth and wonderful learning experience. Building an entire application from scratch with AI tools has provided valuable insights into the modern development workflow and the mindset required to partner effectively with AI. I've learned a great deal about the iterative process of building with AI, the importance of following established conventions, and as always, the critical role of proper planning.

## What Worked Well

The Gemini agent, both in the CLI and within an editor like Zed, performed exceptionally well when given clear and context-rich prompts. I was particularly impressed with Cursor's approach to debugging; it avoided getting stuck in loops and consistently proposed alternative solutions to complex problems. The Context7 feature also proved invaluable for accessing up-to-date syntax and library information, extending the capabilities of the models beyond their training data and keeping them relevant.

This experience has also been a significant confidence booster. The feeling of being discouraged when starting a new project or contributing to an existing one has diminished. I've come to realize that with a solid plan and a clear strategy, any development challenge is surmountable.

## What Felt Limiting

To be truly effective when using AI models, a foundational understanding of the project's core concepts and technologies is essential. Without this context, it's easy to get stuck on errors when implementing more advanced features. More importantly, a lack of understanding can lead to accepting mediocre or vague suggestions from the AI, which can compromise the quality and integrity of the codebase. The AI is a powerful tool, but it's not a replacement for developer knowledge and critical thinking. It's a collaborator, not a magic wand.

## What I Learned

This project reinforced several key principles for working with AI:

-   **Prompting**: The quality of the output is directly proportional to the quality of the input. Never be lazy when writing a prompt; providing clear, concise, and context-aware instructions is paramount.
-   **Reviewing**: Thoroughly reviewing AI-generated suggestions is non-negotiable. This is crucial for catching potential security vulnerabilities, logical errors, and unwanted code patterns. It ensures the final product is robust and secure.
-   **Iterating**: The development process is a continuous cycle of review and improvement. As new insights are gained and requirements evolve, it's necessary to iterate on previous suggestions to maintain high quality across the entire codebase. This iterative feedback loop is what transforms a good start into a great finished product.
